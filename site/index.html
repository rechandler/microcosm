A granular, micro-loop, glitch and echo effect plugin, built for cinematic guitar textures.

Space

Six reverbs, each with its own Character knob

Put the reverb after the effect, before it (so the effect chops up the reverb wash) or on the effect only (your dry guitar stays dry). The Character knob does something different for each reverb. At the centre you get the standard sound.

Glass · AIR

A small, bright room. Turn Air from dull wood to sparkling glass.

Velvet · WARMTH

A medium, dark room. Warmth goes from murky to smooth.

Hall · DECAY

A large hall, with a tail from 2 to 10 seconds.

Haze · MOVEMENT

A long ambient wash. Movement adds wobble, from still to seasick.

Shimmer · SPARKLE

Octave-up pitch shifting inside the tail, from a faint halo to an endlessly rising choir.

Cathedral · BLOOM

Huge and long. Bloom makes each note swell slowly into the space.

Made for playing

Built to sit in your session

Small touches that make Tessera easy to play live and to record with.

Follows Live's transport

Press play and the effect starts fresh, locked to the grid. Press stop and it fades out and forgets. Turn Transport off to keep it running.

Tempo sync

Time locks to Live's tempo as a note value, from 1/32 to a full bar. With Sync off it runs free, from 50 ms to 2 s.

Hold

Freezes what the effect is working on, so it keeps generating from what you just played while you play over it.

Reverse

Plays every loop, grain and echo backwards.

Drift and Lo-Fi

Tape-style wow and flutter, plus old-sampler grit. Turn Lo-Fi past halfway and the loops slow down too, an octave down at full.

Clean pitch shifting

Octave-up loops and grains read from band-limited audio, and pitched echoes line up with your waveform. Sustained notes stay clean.

See what it's doing

Engine View and built-in help

A live display of your playing, with the effect's output on top. Every loop and grain shows as a dot at the moment of your playing it's reading from. Colour and height show its pitch, size shows its level, and rings mean it's playing in reverse. The echoes show as markers at their delay times.

Stuck? Click ? next to the title for a one-page quick reference to every control.

Install

Get it into Ableton Live

WINDOWS

  1. Download Tessera-Windows-x64.zip and unzip it.
  2. Copy Tessera.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  3. In Live, open Options → Preferences → Plug-Ins, turn on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders, then click Rescan.
  4. Find it under Plug-Ins → Tessera Audio → Tessera.

The plugin isn't code-signed, so Windows Security may flag it as unknown the first time. In Windows Security → Protection history, choose Allow on device.

MACOS

  1. Download Tessera-macOS-Universal.zip and unzip it.
  2. Copy Tessera.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and/or Tessera.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  3. In Live, enable VST3 and/or Audio Units under Preferences → Plug-Ins and rescan.

The build isn't notarized. If macOS blocks it, run this in Terminal:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Tessera.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Tessera.component
